## Strength over time

Estimated 1RM from the best set each month for squat, bench, and deadlift, using the [Epley formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-repetition_maximum#Epley_formula):

`e1RM = w × (1 + r / 30)`

where `w` is the weight and `r` is the reps. So a set of 225 × 8 estimates to about 285 lb. It's a rule of thumb, not a measured max — it works best in the ~2–10 rep range and tends to overestimate on high-rep sets. Here it's just a way to compare sets across different rep counts on one scale.
